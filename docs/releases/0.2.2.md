## Kit 0.2.2

One failed call no longer raises the "server isn't answering" banner.

- **Debounced.** The banner shows after 2 failed calls in a row, or 5 seconds after a failure with no call answered since. Any answered call ends the count. (Since 0.2.1, a 502–504 carrying the app's own `x-<app id>` header is the server's error, not a down server.)
- **Cancelled calls are ignored.** An aborted call, or one cut off because the page is being left, raises nothing and fails with "The request was cancelled".
