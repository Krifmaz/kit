## Kit 0.6.0

Apps on the kit can now let people sign up.

- **Who can sign up.** An admin picks Closed (only admins make accounts), Invite only (the default) or Open.
- **Invites.** One-time or multi-use links with an expiry, a most uses, and the role and presets the new account gets. Revoke one at any time, and see who used each. A link is shown once, when it is made.
- **Open sign-up.** Limits per address and per day, and, if wanted, an email confirmation and an admin's approval. Accounts that wait can't sign in, and say why.
- **One setting for a suite.** When apps share accounts, the app that keeps them decides, and the others show its setting read-only and send people there.
- **Every client.** The sign-up page, the line under sign-in, and the admin page on web, desktop and Android, in the template and its demos.

New crate: `kit-server-signup`. The app keeps its own accounts; the kit asks it to make them.
