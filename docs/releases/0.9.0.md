## Kit 0.9.0

A design system, mobile first, for web and Android: apps built on the kit look rich and work on phones.

- **Made for phones first.** Below 768 px an app gets a top app bar and a bottom tab bar (four or five sections, the rest under More), full-width sheets instead of dialogs, 44 px touch targets and room for notches and the home indicator. Tablets get a navigation rail, wider screens the sidebar. Nothing scrolls sideways except the rows meant to.
- **Rich pages.** Poster and cover grids with hover and press states, hero banners with blurred backdrop art, carousels ("Trending", "Recently added"), detail pages (backdrop, cover, metadata chips, actions), stat cards, progress rings, empty states with illustrations, skeletons while loading and smooth page transitions.
- **Polish.** One spacing and type scale, subtle shadows and glass bars on the Encore palette, Lucide icons everywhere, animations under 200 ms, and none at all when the system asks for reduced motion.
- **Checked on every change.** CI screenshots every demo page on a phone, a tablet and a desktop, puts the pictures on the pull request, and fails when anything overflows sideways or text overlaps.

Web: kit-leptos `ui` (`AppShell`, `Sheet`, `MediaCard`, `Carousel`, `Hero`, …). Android: kit-compose `ui` (`KitScaffold`, `KitSheet`, `MediaCard`, `Carousel`, `Hero`, …) and `KitIcons`.
