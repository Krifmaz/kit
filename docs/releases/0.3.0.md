## Kit 0.3.0

Apps on the kit can now work as one suite.

- **Manifest.** Every app can say what it is at `/api/kit/manifest`: its name, icon, sections with icons, address, deep-link patterns and search. All of it comes from the app; the kit names no app.
- **Linked apps.** An admin makes a key in one app and pastes it with that app's address into another. The other app's sections then show in the shared sidebar, and a click opens that app at that section.
- **Your sidebar.** Everyone can hide, show and reorder the sidebar's entries, from every linked app; the choice is kept with their account.
- **One sign-in.** One app keeps the accounts; the others trust it through OpenID Connect, so people sign in there once.
- **One search.** A search asks every linked app at once, and an app can show "related in …" links from the others.

New crate: `kit-server-suite`. The screens for web, desktop and Android follow in the next release.
