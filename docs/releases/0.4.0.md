## Kit 0.4.0

Linked apps now show up in every app's screens.

- **Shared sidebar.** The sidebar on the web, desktop and Android shows this app's pages and every linked app's.
  Edit sidebar lets each person move, hide and show entries. A click opens this app's page in place, or the
  other app at that section.
- **Search.** One search box asks this app and every linked app at once. An app that can't be reached says why
  in one line, and the others still answer. Related in … shows what other apps have for a page.
- **Sign in with ….** The sign-in page offers the account source when one is linked.
- **Linked apps settings.** Admins make keys, link apps and choose the account source from Settings → Linked apps.
- **Template.** New apps are suite members from the start, and two copies link to each other out of the box.
